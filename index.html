FAQ

Questions contractors ask

What counts as one AI plan read?

Uploading a drawing set (or a new revision) and letting the AI read it is one read. Opening the report, asking questions and marking work done don't use reads.

How accurate is the AI count?

It reads the plans, legends and schedules the way an estimator does, and lists anything unclear as a question for the engineer. Always double-check counts before you order material or bid.

Do my workers need to pay?

No. One plan covers your whole company — add as many employees as you need.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel in Settings → Plan & billing. If you cancel during the 30-day trial, you are never charged.

Does it work on the phone?

Yes. VoltTrack runs in the browser on any iPhone, Android or computer — nothing to install.
